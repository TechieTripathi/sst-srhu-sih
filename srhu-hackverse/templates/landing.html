SRHU Brand Philosophy

At Swami Rama Himalayan University, our guiding philosophy is direction, purpose, holistic mentorship, and experiential transformation. TechForge 3.0 represents this compass in engineering education—guiding aspiring technologists from conceptual problems to robust, deployed innovations.

36-Hour Hackathon Sprint

Intense prototyping sprint testing resilience, software architecture, and real-time execution across 6 checkpoints.

Institutional Integrity

Functional Prototype Focus

SIH 2026 Fast-Track

Institutional Standards

SIH 2026 Team Requirements

To qualify for official nomination to the Smart India Hackathon 2026, registered teams must strictly adhere to the regulatory composition and originality guidelines.

6

Exactly 6 Student Members

Every team must consist of exactly 6 currently enrolled undergraduate/postgraduate students of SST, SRHU.

♀

Mandatory Female Representation

In strict alignment with SIH national guidelines, each team must include at least one female student member.

👑

Designated Team Leader

One Team Leader serves as the primary liaison for registration, announcements, schedule management, and submissions.

⚙️

Working Prototype Mandatory

Theoretical presentations or slide-only ideas are disqualified. Teams must demonstrate a tangible proof-of-concept.

🔒

Single Team Enrollment

A student can belong to only one registered team. Cross-registration attempts are invalidated by the system.

📜

Responsible Tech & Attribution

Full disclosure of pre-existing code, open-source libraries, APIs, and AI-assisted tooling is mandatory during evaluation.

Institutional Tie-Breaking Protocol

In the event of identical composite scores between teams, tie resolution follows the strict priority order: (1) Prototype & Implementation score, followed by (2) Technical Design & Feasibility, and then (3) Innovation & Differentiation.
